# Research scope and data quality — November 10, 2026

**Not a certified all-time record book.** The website is a working research and discovery prototype containing 180 documented performance entries plus a separate 2024 Alvernia men's indoor PDF archive for four events. In this update, 77 additional entries were transcribed from the **2026 outdoor** TFRRS team *Top Performances* pages. They are seasonal top marks, **not all-time places**.

## Collection inventory

| Program | Indoor entries | Outdoor entries |
|---|---:|---:|
| Men | 9 (+ 2024 top-ten PDF) | 54 |
| Women | 36 | 81 |

These are raw sourced entries, **not counts of verified school records**. Many events have fewer than ten recovered marks. The site computes ranks only **within the recovered collection**. It does not imply a true historical top 10 for every event.

## Critical remaining work before publication as definitive school records and all-time rankings

1. Obtain all seasons for both teams from TFRRS or an authorized season-level export; the current TFRRS team default displays **2026**, not all program history.
2. Verify a school-record holder in each event against the whole timeline, not just the latest-season leader; do not equate historical **record when set** with **record currently standing**.
3. Compare every legal meet result for all historical years, accounting for wind-aided sprint/jump marks, indoor/outdoor differences, event specifications, timing precision, duplicate relay squads and conversions.
4. Confirm relay team member names and all-time 4×100 records with the athletics record keeper; the 2026 men's 43.50 and women's 52.37 are confirmed **season marks only**.
5. Have athletics staff approve a final official record book and mark its review date.

The 2026 women’s 11.21m outdoor triple jump mark has a **+3.0 m/s wind reading** on the TFRRS list and is excluded from the site's legal-ranking computation; it is retained in the CSV with a wind-aided flag for historical transparency.
