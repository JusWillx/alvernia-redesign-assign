# TFRRS-based historical leaderboard methodology

TFRRS performances are sufficient evidence for this student project; entries do **not** require separate confirmation by Alvernia Athletics. `RANKED_TOP10_RESEARCH.csv` contains both sexes and both seasons and ranks legal marks gathered so far for every event. It includes the historical Alvernia men's indoor 2024 all-time archive, individual historical source research, and a TFRRS 2026 outdoor performance list.

**Rank 1 = leading mark among collected results** and a school-record *candidate*, **not** a certified historical record until earlier TFRRS seasons are collected. A list with fewer than 10 marks is incomplete. Repeated athletes count as repeated performances, not unique people. Marks with known illegal wind assistance are excluded. No conversion or automatic indoor/outdoor mixing was performed.

To make a definitive historical top ten, the next data-collection step is exporting each TFRRS season (men/women, indoor/outdoor) for every year of the program and combining the same event standards. This environment cannot directly crawl the full TFRRS archive; the site therefore does not pretend it has done so.

Sources: https://www.tfrrs.org/teams/tf/PA_college_m_Alvernia.html and https://www.tfrrs.org/teams/tf/PA_college_f_Alvernia.html plus linked row sources.

## November 10 correction: Cross-year data and historical visibility
The initial exported ranking over-weighted 2026 because the only bulk outdoor season list ingested was 2026. This revision adds TFRRS 2022 men’s outdoor event lists, 2024–2025 TFRRS athlete profile marks (including women’s indoor triple jump), and 2024/2025 relays. Each event now includes an expandable pre-2026 results table even when those older marks miss the ten best. Year/date placeholders on athlete college-best rows are labeled as such and must not be interpreted as verified meet dates. This does not claim all historical seasons have been ingested.

TFRRS 2022: https://www.tfrrs.org/top_performances/PA_college_m_Alvernia.html?list_hnd=3730&season_hnd=568
