# Track & Field Historical Performance Research

## Research scope
Research date: November 10, 2026. This independent site aims to recover historically noteworthy marks even when Alvernia's official current records page does not present them.

**99 sourced additional performance rows** were added across men's/women's and indoor/outdoor disciplines. The previously transcribed men's 2024 indoor all-time archive is also retained for four events. Individual repeated performances are intentionally kept as separate rankings when the meet/date/mark differs.

## Important integrity limits
- **This is not yet a certified all-time top-ten record book.** Only four men’s indoor events have a published historical top-ten baseline in the implemented comparison. Every other event ranks only among the sourced results recovered into this local dataset, so #1 is NOT necessarily the true school record.
- The *“Record when set”* label is historical; some marks were overtaken by later results. Do not present these historical record milestones as the current record holder.
- The archive does not yet cover every season, meet, event, or legal wind/track conversion detail. Some sources summarize season bests but omit full date/meet details, in which case the site uses the reporting season as the date.
- Cross-check original results before claiming a complete ranking, and ensure outdoor sprint/jump results were wind legal.
- Athlete names, event nomenclature, and relay membership follow source spelling. The archive also includes multiple marks by the same person, rather than just one PR per athlete.
- No code here changes Alvernia Athletics' official site.
