# Historical audit (November 10, 2026)

**Scope requested:** all men/women indoor/outdoor events and relays, founding through 2026. **Status:** not complete. The project CSV is an evidence sample, not a complete season-by-season extraction.

The archive contains 298 individually documented performance rows; 39 new rows in this revision were transcribed from TFRRS athlete histories. Older seasons are not comprehensively represented, and missing years must not be interpreted as no competitions.

## What is missing

Full TFRRS season exports or results for the years listed in `SEASON_COVERAGE.csv`, plus validation of every relay squad, wind legality and event equivalence. Some TFRRS athlete pages show relay marks without full member lists; those are identified as partial squads.
