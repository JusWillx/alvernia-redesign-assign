# Alvernia Track & Field: earliest season and historical coverage

**Founding year:** the first documented Alvernia indoor track & field meet was December 5, 2009, at Ursinus College. The first outdoor season was spring 2010. Both men’s and women’s early track records are evidenced by the 2010–11 reports. The audit window is **2009–2026**.
